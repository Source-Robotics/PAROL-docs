# Inspection

With the robot switched off and the supply unplugged:

| Part | What to do | Right is | If not |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Belts** (joints 1, 3, 4, 5) | Press each with a finger through its cover opening. Look for frayed edges and missing teeth. | It gives a little and springs back | A belt that sags or that you can push against the pulley needs tensioning ([Belts](belts.md)) |
| **Couplers and set screws** | Hold the output of each joint and try to turn the motor side | Only the gearbox's backlash | Any other play is a loose set screw ([Couplers and threadlocker](couplers.md)) |
| **Limit switches and sensors** | Press each switch lever; bring a screw head near each inductive sensor | The switch clicks; the sensor's LED lights | |
| **Printed parts** | Look at the screw bosses and bearing seats, especially on the elbow and the upper arm, and at the forearm | No cracks; no warping of the forearm from heat | |
| **Cables and tubes** | Look where the arm's cables pass through the joints, and where the gripper's cable and tubes cross joints 5 and 6 from the forearm | No chafing; the gripper cable's connector tight | |
| **The fan** | Check it with the robot on, and look at the intake | It turns; the intake is free of dust | A stopped fan overheats the drivers |
| **The base** | Check the mounting bolts and the E-stop button | The bolts tight; the button firm in its housing | |

Then:

1. Switch on.
2. Home, and watch the homing.

!!! success "Check"
    Each joint backs off and re-approaches its switch cleanly, and the pose at the end matches the witness marks
    ([Checking the homing](homing-check.md)).

## Cleaning

Clean the robot switched off and unplugged.

| What | With | Never |
|---|---|---|
| The printed parts | A dry or slightly damp cloth, or a little isopropyl alcohol on the cloth for grease | Other solvents |
| The base | | Spray anything into it: the control board and the fan are open to it |
| The fan intake | A soft brush or a vacuum cleaner | Compressed air, which drives the dust into the board |
