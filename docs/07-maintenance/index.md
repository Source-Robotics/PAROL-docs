# 7. Maintenance

PAROL6 needs little: belts that stay tight, set screws that stay put, switches that trigger. Check them at the
intervals below and after any collision.

| Interval | What |
|---|---|
| Before each session | The E-stop works (press it with the robot enabled); nothing in the workspace; the cables and tubes free |
| Monthly, or every 50 hours | [Inspection](inspection.md): belts, couplers, switches, fan, printed parts |
| After a collision, a fall or a lost step | Inspection, then [Checking the homing](homing-check.md) |
| When a belt slips or a joint is loose | [Belts](belts.md), [Couplers and threadlocker](couplers.md) |
