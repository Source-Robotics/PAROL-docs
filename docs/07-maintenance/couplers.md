# Couplers and threadlocker

The motor shafts, the gearbox outputs and the joint 6 shaft are joined by couplers and pulleys with set screws on
flats or keys. Vibration loosens set screws. A loose one lets the motor turn without the joint, and the robot
loses its position without noticing.

## A joint that slips

If a joint starts to slip, or its position drifts between homings:

1. Find the set screw.
2. Tighten it, and apply **blue (medium) threadlocker**. Where the part has both a key and a flat, put a screw on
   the key as well as on the flat.
3. Do not move the robot for 24 hours while it cures.
4. Home the robot and check the witness marks.

## Where threadlocker goes

| Where | Threadlocker |
|---|---|
| Every metal-to-metal screw joint on the robot: the motor mounts, the gearbox flanges, the bearing clamps | Blue (medium) |
| Printed parts and brass inserts | None: they do not need it, and the solvent in some threadlockers attacks plastic. Keep it on the metal. |
| Anywhere | Never red (permanent): the parts cannot be taken apart again without heat |

After tightening a coupler, home the robot and check the witness marks.
