# 10. Transport, storage and disposal

## Moving the robot

1. Home it, then jog joints 2 and 3 toward their limit switches so the arm folds over the base, or move it by
   hand once it is switched off ([Moving the robot by hand](02-safety/moving-by-hand.md)).
2. Switch off with the forearm held; unplug the supply, the USB and the E-stop.
3. Carry it by the base, not by the arm.

## Packing

Fold the arm as above, put foam under the joint 3 limit switch so that the forearm rests on it and not on the
switch, and tape the forearm to the upper arm so that the joints cannot swing. Pack the robot upright in a box
with foam around the base; the gripper and the E-stop separately.

## Storage

Dry, at room temperature, folded. The stepper motors and the drivers have no shelf-life issues; the PETG parts
should not be stored where it gets hot, in a car or near a radiator. Before the first use after storage, run the
[Inspection](08-maintenance/inspection.md).

## Disposal

Separate the parts: the printed PETG is recyclable where PETG is collected; the motors, gearboxes, bearings and
shafts are scrap metal; the control board, the drivers, the power supply and the cables are electronic waste and
go to an electronics collection point, as the law in your country requires. Nothing in the robot is hazardous
waste.
